About Life Care Clinic

Life Care Clinic is a modern healthcare center providing quality medical services with advanced technology and experienced doctors. Our clinic focuses on patient safety, quick diagnosis, and efficient treatment through a digital Medical Appointment and Queue Management System.

Our Mission

Our mission is to deliver compassionate healthcare services with high standards of medical excellence. We aim to reduce patient waiting time by using an online appointment and token-based queue system.

Our Vision

To become a trusted healthcare provider by combining technology and patient-centered care for faster and smarter hospital management.

Project Features

• Online Appointment Booking • Token-Based Queue System • Doctor and Department Management • Patient Information Storage using SQLite Database • Simple and User-Friendly Dashboard Interface
